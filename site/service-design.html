Electrical design.

Electrical plans in ePlan or WSCAD, delivered on paper and in electronic format.

New builds and reconstructions.

ELCON draws up electrical plans for its clients, typically based on machine drawings.

We also produce plans for conversions and reconstructions. Here we often start from old plans that are not yet in electronic format. These are either redrawn or simply supplemented with additional pages.

Cabinets we design.

Power distribution cabinets

Main switch, taps and energy analysers, connected via wired or bus connections such as Rittal RiLine or EATON SASY. Usually designed with Siemens Sentron and linked to the PLC over PROFINET or MODBUS TCP/IP.

Motor cabinets

Motor protections, contactors, SIMOCODE smart relays, soft starters and frequency converters, dimensioned for motor power, intermittency and cable length.

Instrument control cabinets

Where more safety is required: safety PLC IO modules, the right number of contactors, and SIL2 safety functions built into Siemens inverters over PROFISAFE.

Network cabinets

Switches, patch panels and servers installed to the required IP protection level, with climate control if needed, every component labeled with electrical and computer names.

Every area of the plant.

Most often with equipment from SIEMENS and EATON for motor protection and contactors, SIEMENS, ABB and DANFOSS for frequency converters, and SIEMENS and SOLCON for soft starters.
